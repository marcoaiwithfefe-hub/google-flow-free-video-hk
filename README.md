# Google Flow 免費 AI 片：5 條辦公室 prompt

免費 Google 帳號每日有 50 點數，用 Veo 3.1 Lite 一條 8 秒片用 10 點，即係每日 5 條。以下資料 2026 年 11 月 11 日喺 Google 自己嘅說明頁對過。

## 開始之前

1. 打開 https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow ，用你嘅 Google 帳號登入
2. 帳號要年滿 18 歲，未驗證年齡會叫你驗
3. 揀影片，模型揀 **Veo 3.1 Lite**，長度揀 8 秒
4. 下面 prompt 成段 copy，`[ ]` 入面換做你自己嘅嘢
5. 夜晚大約 10 點至 1 點（香港時間）免費帳戶可能出唔到片，相照出到，避開呢段時間
6. 生成失敗或者俾安全過濾擋咗，點數一個鐘內退返
7. 當日用唔晒嘅點數唔會留到聽日

Prompt 用英文效果最好，係 Google 自己講嘅。

## 5 條 prompt

### 1. 新產品出 post

```
An 8-second product video of [a matte white water bottle with a teal cap] standing on a round yellow pedestal in a bright studio. The camera slowly orbits halfway around the product at eye level. Soft daylight from the left, a plain light grey background, small paper confetti falling in the last two seconds. No text, no people.
```

### 2. 活動預告

```
An 8-second teaser for [a company workshop] in [a modern office meeting room with a long wooden table and a city view]. The room starts empty with chairs pushed in; the camera dollies forward from the door to the window while the lights switch on one row at a time. Warm evening light outside. No text, no faces.
```

### 3. 簡報開場

```
An 8-second opening shot for a presentation about [quarterly sales]. A clean desk with a laptop, a notebook and a cup of coffee, seen from above. The camera slowly pushes down toward the laptop screen, which shows [a simple rising bar chart]. Calm, even office light. No readable text on the screen.
```

### 4. 節日問候

```
An 8-second festive greeting video for [Lunar New Year]. A reception desk in [a small Hong Kong office] decorated with [red lanterns and a mandarin orange plant]. The camera glides slowly from left to right past the decorations. Soft warm light, gentle movement in the lanterns. No text, no people.
```

### 5. 歡迎新同事

```
An 8-second welcome video for a new team member. An empty desk by a window in [an open-plan office], with a potted plant, a new laptop and a welcome card standing on the keyboard. The camera moves slowly from the aisle toward the desk and stops on the card. Bright morning light. No readable text, no people.
```

## 出片之後

- 唔啱心水就改一樣嘢再出（鏡頭、光、背景），唔好成段重寫
- 片上面可能有可見嘅 AI 水印，Google 亦會用 SynthID 標記係 AI 整
- 片入面嘅產品、地方、數字，發出去之前自己睇清楚
